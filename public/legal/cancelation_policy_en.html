Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellation

At Xeve.ai, we strive to provide our users with a seamless experience. If you wish to cancel your account or subscriptions, please follow the steps below:

How to Cancel Your Account

To cancel your account, follow these steps:

  1. Go to the main page of xeve.ai.
  2. Click on the gear icon located in the top navigation bar.
  3. Select the "Delete Account" option and follow the instructions provided.

How to Cancel a Subscription

To cancel a subscription associated with your account:

  1. Navigate to the "My AIs" section from your dashboard.
  2. Find the subscription you wish to cancel.
  3. Click the "Cancel" button next to the specific subscription.

Once the cancellation process is completed, you will receive a confirmation email. If you encounter any issues, please contact our support team at support@xeve.ai.

Refund Policy

At Xeve.ai, we operate on a subscription-based model. Refunds are generally not provided for partial billing periods. When you cancel a subscription, you will retain access to the service until the end of your current billing cycle, and no further charges will be applied.

If you believe you are eligible for a refund due to exceptional circumstances, please contact our support team at support@xeve.ai. Refund requests will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
